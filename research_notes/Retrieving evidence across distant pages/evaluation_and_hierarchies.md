# Evaluation and retrieval hierarchies

## What does the repository actually establish about distant evidence?

### Takeaway
The five-question slice is a useful failure alarm, but it is not yet a validated coreference benchmark: selection requires only a large span between existing relevance labels. Some selected questions ask for one fact that might have multiple valid source locations. The existing tests therefore do not establish that two distant pages are jointly necessary.

### Inferences
- Before another model experiment, annotate complete necessary evidence sets; distinguish acceptable alternative sets from facts that must all be combined. Define success as at least one complete valid set retrieved, not retrieval of every place the answer happens to occur.
- A reranker can recover rank errors inside a candidate pool, but cannot recover an absent page. Attribute failures to candidate omission, reranker removal, parent/page conversion, or reader truncation before changing ingestion.
- Different mentions of Brandon Stark are a useful entity-conflation stress test. A document-wide alias string dictionary that merges names without identity evidence can make this case worse.

## What evidence supports RAPTOR, proposition retrieval and bounded follow-up retrieval?

### Takeaway
Standalone propositions have a direct mechanism for missing referents and can be indexed in ordinary pgvector rows, but their published evidence is mostly Wikipedia with older embeddings. RAPTOR has real book/scientific-paper QA evidence; independent long-book comparisons show small, zero or negative gains in some settings, so it should rank below explicit mention linking for this user's exact defect.

### Cited Findings
- **Propositions / Dense X Retrieval, author evidence, 2023/2024.** The paper defines atomic, self-contained units with resolved context/coreferences. It indexes propositions separately, retrieves extra units, then maps them to unique source passages—compatible with search-small/return-page. Its Wikipedia corpus has 256.9M propositions vs 41.4M passages (about 6.2x units), illustrating potential index/call multiplication. The propositionizer was Flan-T5-large distilled from 42k GPT-4-generated training passages. This is not a demonstration that arbitrary 2B–9B models can safely resolve book-wide identities. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06648v2)
- Its strongest general pattern was larger gains for weaker/unsupervised retrieval; GTR average passage-level Recall@5 across fiveQA datasets goes65.2->68.0 using propositions. This is not a guarantee for Gemini Embedding2, novels, legal or medical text, and the paper's recall is answer-containing passage retrieval, not complete far-evidence recall. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.06648v2)
- **RAPTOR, author evidence, ICLR2024.** Recursive cluster/embed/summarize retrieval was evaluated on NarrativeQA(books/scripts), QASPER(scientific papers), and QuALITY(medium-length passages). Controlled SBERT+UnifiedQA3B NarrativeQA ROUGE goes29.26->30.87; QASPER with GPT-4 yields55.7F1,2.7points over DPR. Its supplementary scalability experiment covers roughly12.5k–78k tokens, not proof of all750-page workloads. These are answer metrics, not exact source-page pair recall. [RAPTOR](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.18059v1)
- **Independent evaluation, Han et al., RAG vs GraphRAG2025(updated version).** In its NovelQA table with Llama3.1-8B, aggregate accuracy is57.12 RAG,57.12 RAPTOR,56.54 HippoRAG2; multi-hop accuracy47.34/48.17/47.84 respectively. Unified setup uses~256token chunks, ada-002 embeddings, and GPT-4o-mini graph extraction, so it cannot certify the user's local2B–9B+Gemini configuration. Its token-matched MultiHop-RAG comparison with70B closes vanilla RAG to71.01 vs71.17 Community-GraphRAG, illustrating the need to control evidence budget. [Independent comparison](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.11371v3)
- **Independent evaluation, Xiang et al., GraphRAG-Bench, ICLR2026.** Appendix Table5 on its novel corpus: reranked RAG fact accuracy60.92, complex reasoning42.93, contextual summarization51.30; HippoRAG2:60.14/53.38/64.10; LightRAG:58.62/49.07/48.85; MS-GraphRAG local:49.29/50.93/64.40; RAPTOR:49.25/38.59/47.10. Graph reasoning gains coexist with detail losses. Corpus includes Gutenberg novels and NCCN medical guidelines; generated questions/LLM judging and configuration remain limits. This is not the different textbook benchmark also named GraphRAG-Bench(arxiv2506.02404). [Independent benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05690v3)
- The same benchmark's token table reports a large range of prompt costs; its nearby explanatory prose does not numerically agree with the table. Avoid universal dollar claims from it. The defensible finding is that implementation and task produce very different costs, while compact HippoRAG2 and summary/global approaches need separate measurement. [Benchmark efficiency section](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05690v3)
- **Bounded iterative retrieval.** The independent Han comparison uses IRCoT and finds iterative retrieval and reranking generally improve multi-hop QA beyond the underlying architecture. That supports trying an additional evidence-seeking retrieval round, but does not itself show100-page coreference resolution with a2B model. [Independent study](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.11371v3)
- **NovelQA, ICLR2025, benchmark evidence.** It includes89novels and2,305expert-annotated questions, varied single/multi-hop/detail questions, evidence positions and distance analyses. It is closer to the user's domain than Wikipedia-only tasks, but printed-page labels and exact pronoun-bridge questions still need custom validation. [NovelQA](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12766)
- **NovelHopQA, EMNLP2025, benchmark evidence.** This evaluates1–4hops over64k–128ktoken excerpts from83public-domain novels, with keyword-guided chains, oracle-context checks and human validation. It supplies useful construction patterns rather than a plug-in, independent proof that one proposed retrieval fix works. [NovelHopQA](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1328.pdf)
- **MultiHop-RAG, COLM2024.** Its corpus is English news articles with gold supporting evidence. Use as an out-of-domain sanity check, not as evidence of book-level pronoun chains or printed-page separation. [MultiHop-RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15391)

### Inferences
- **Selective standalone search views**: for chunks genuinely missing a referent, create a concise supported proposition such as“Arun[entityE7] sold the farm in spring” pointing to the original sentence/page plus the separate identity evidence. Keep negation, time, uncertainty, quantities, attribution and modality. Search the view and return the raw source. Start with a maximum of one view per affected chunk to bound embedding/index multiplication, not all propositions everywhere. This is a recommendation inferred from mechanism and partial evidence, not a published tested winner under the user's setup.
- One vector per API text does not mean one vector per source chunk forever. Ordinary extra pgvector rows with`representation_type` and`source_chunk_id` support separate views. Fusion must cap/dedup by original page before finalK; otherwise extra rows buy unfairly many chances and one page crowds out the second.
- A short parent/chapter summary can route search to a distant chapter but cannot be trusted to preserve a minor event. RAPTOR needs summary->all descendant source links and raw leaf retrieval before citations. A summary mention with50descendant pages is not success at finding an exact evidence page.
- **Bounded follow-up**: retrieve initially, identify which requested relation/event is still unsupported, use only retrieved/verified entity aliases to perform one or two additional searches, then take a fixed-size page union. This is cheap if occasional and effective when the first hit reveals a usable bridge. It fails when both first-stage hits are wrong or no name link was indexed; a model must not invent the alias or infer identity from gender/role alone.
- Cost should be stated as incremental generation tokens/GPU seconds plus actual extra embedding text. Proposition expansion can multiply units by several times; hierarchical summaries add nodes at every level; follow-up adds1–2query embeddings/searches and optionally1–2local small-model calls. No source establishes dollars for this exact workload; use the repo's measured local GPU speed as planning evidence, not portable guaranteed prices.

### Gaps
- I found no independent controlled study demonstrating an off-the-shelf method fixes name-to-“old man” retrieval across100printed pages, across all requested domains, with Google embedding2 and2B–9B enrichment within a few dollars.
- No universal requirement exists that a pronoun's antecedent be100pages back. Often local coreference chains connect to a document-wide identity. Evaluate chain quality separately from evidence-page distance.

## How should the fix be tested and how many questions are needed?

### Takeaway
Use a frozen, human-verified test split selected without looking at any system's misses, score complete source evidence before and after reranking, and make document-level generalization explicit. Start with~50development questions, then~200–300held-out questions across at least15–20documents; smaller effects and domain-specific claims need more.

### Cited Findings
- Exact small-sample binomial intervals and Wilson intervals avoid inappropriate symmetric normal intervals at0or1. [NIST interval methods](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm)
- For paired binary outcomes, exact McNemar uses the discordant pairs and a binomial distribution. This matches“baseline fails, fix succeeds” versus“baseline succeeds, fix fails” on the same questions. It is not an unpaired comparison of two proportions. [Statsmodels official McNemar documentation](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.contingency_tables.mcnemar.html)
- Calculated locally through `UV_CACHE_DIR=/private/tmp/rag-research-uv uv run --no-sync python -c ...` using existing SciPy:95%Clopper–Pearson0/5=[0,.52182375];2/5=[.05274495,.85336720]. These are calculations applying the NIST binomial method, not results of a new retrieval run.

### Inferences
**Gold construction, concrete sequence:**
1. Sample documents and document positions first, across novel/thesis/financial/medical/legal/policy strata; hold out entire documents from tuning. Do not write the benchmark by selecting only current-system failures. Permit an explicitly labelled failure-analysis slice separately.
2. A human selects a real later event/claim where a name is absent or ambiguous, finds the text establishing identity, and records exact quotes, physical PDF page index, displayed page label(if different), token offsets, source version/hash, entity IDs and each link's evidence. For reports, use defined-term/acronym, company/subsidiary, treatment/cohort, clause/exceptions or policy referents, not only personal names.
3. Write a natural question naming the entity and asking for a synthesis that genuinely needs both distant evidence regions, e.g.“How does Arun's promise in the early agreement compare with the action later attributed to the old man?” Verify both facts belong to the same entity. Also test one-page questions whose later page has only a pronoun; those measure resolution but should not falsely require the first-page citation if the identity is already established elsewhere.
4. Two annotators, blind to systems' outputs, verify answer, identity and necessity. Delete either evidence region and check that the complete answer is no longer justified from remaining evidence. Use an oracle reader only as an additional screen, with human judgement deciding ambiguity. Gold should represent alternative complete support sets where the same facts are repeated; each is acceptable. Resolve disagreements before scoring.
5. Retain entity-name-on-both-pages controls, one-page questions, ambiguous same-name negatives, changing roles/temporality, unanswerable questions, tables/footnotes, and extraction failures. Report at least token distance and relative document positions alongside the>=100physical-page slice, since printed-page distance is not comparable across formats. No need to falsify natural document pages merely to hit the threshold.

**Fixed ablation and measures:**
- Freeze extractor/chunker/raw corpus, title prefix, Google model+dimension+task format, index/search settings, hybrid weight, thresholds, random seeds and reranker/model/text budget. Preserve unchanged raw-only baseline. Use distinct manifests for old-branch vs current-main results.
- Compare baseline, baseline+verified explicit alias/name-page index, baseline+resolved mention index, baseline+selective standalone view, baseline+one bounded follow-up; graph/hierarchy methods only after this minimal comparison. For an oracle diagnostic arm, supply human-correct identity links—if that does not lift pair recall, entity resolution is not the principal bottleneck.
- Hold the candidate page budget, reranker input budget and final unique-page/token budget constant. Match total retrieval/scoring work as far as possible and separately show incremental cost; do not allow an auxiliary index or graph to double candidate slots unnoticed. Include a deeper raw-only retrieval control at the same compute budget.
- Primary: `complete_evidence@10` over unique raw source pages, accepting any human-validated complete support set. With exactly2necessary pages and no alternatives this equals pair-recall/both-pages@10. Also show@20and all-support recall, first-stage candidate complete coverage, final after-reranker complete coverage, and proportion of retrieved source text actually visible to the reader. Page deduplication occurs before applyingK. Report normal one-page accuracy/regressions, not only target failures.
- Run every ingestion arm both without and with the same existing reranker. Preserve scores at all stages: candidate absence cannot be repaired by reranking; candidate present/final absent is a ranking/selection issue. Audit whether the reranker receives the canonical identity label; a raw pronoun-only pair can be incorrectly downranked even after correct entity retrieval.
- Independently audit extraction/link precision and recall on random mentions: alias quote support is not identity-link precision, and a quote validating two strings does not prove they denote the same entity. Count abstained unresolved mentions and wrong-entity merges. Generated views can route retrieval; answers/citations must be supported by the original text and the identity evidence.
- Bootstrap paired differences by document(and optionally questions within documents), report per-domain/per-document effects and wins/losses. With only2documents no method can estimate cross-domain transfer credibly, regardless of the number of questions from them. Question-level exact McNemar can be a descriptive conditional test, but its independence assumption is inadequate for broad claims when questions cluster within books. Choose one primary contrast/metric before seeing test data; multiple exploratory variants get multiplicity correction or clear exploratory labels.

**Counts and uncertainty, concrete planning values:**
- The0/5and2/5rates have huge exact intervals above. Five paired questions cannot produce two-sided exactMcNemarp<.05even if all five improve and none worsen:minimum p=2/2^5=.0625. Repeating identical deterministic queries does not create new observations.
- For estimating a single success probability near50%, about100independent questions gives95%half-width~9.8percentage points;~400gives~4.9points. These are precision calculations and are not power guarantees for detecting a difference.
- For paired improvement, letq=probability the two systems disagree anddelta=net absolute improvement. Normal-approximation planning at two-sidedalpha=.05,power=.80 is `n≈(1.96*sqrt(q)+.842*sqrt(q-delta^2))^2/delta^2`. This follows from the paired binary difference taking values-1,0,+1, with varianceq-delta^2; use the pilot discordance rates and simulate the exact planned test before fixingn. It is a derived approximation, not an empirical claim from a RAG paper.
- Locally computed values:delta=.15,q=.25 ->85independent questions;delta=.10,q=.25 ->194;delta=.10,q=.30 ->234;delta=.05,q=.30 ->940. Clustering, many variants and domain-level conclusions increase the requirement. Therefore~50devplus200–300frozen test is a sensible first decision for a meaningful~10point improvement if discordance is~25–30%, with broad document coverage and honest intervals. If seeking5points, do not pretend200is enough;~1,000may be needed even before clustering. If budget/annotation limits allow only100, aim to diagnose large improvements and report uncertainty.
- At least several independent documents per domain are needed before claiming that domain;15–20totaldocuments and200–300queries is a starting engineering study, not proof of universal parity across all six domains. Report domain counts and avoid publishing thin per-domain point estimates as settled results.
